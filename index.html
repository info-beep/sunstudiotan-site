Frequently asked questions

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are recommended. We're open daily, 8 AM to 8 PM. Book online at book.sunstudiotan.com or call or text (614) 333-0051.

How long does an airbrush spray tan last?

Most airbrush spray tans last 7 to 10 days, depending on your skin, your prep, and your aftercare. Color fully develops within about 24 hours.

How should I prep for my spray tan?

Exfoliate the day before, shave or wax at least 8 hours ahead, and arrive with clean skin: no lotion, deodorant, or makeup. Wear loose, dark clothing.

Do you use tanning beds or UV?

No. Sun Studio Tan is 100% sunless. Every tan is hand-applied by airbrush using Norvell, Venetian, and heated Evolv solutions.

How long does LED teeth whitening take?

A Bleach Bright LED teeth whitening session takes about 30 minutes, right in the studio.

Where do I park?

Park at The Joseph Garage on Russell St and bring your ticket in. We validate 2 hours of free parking at checkout.
